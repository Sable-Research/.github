Sable Research was founded by experienced offensive security researchers.

## Research Acquisition Program

We maintain an ongoing **Exploit Acquisition Program** for independent security researchers.

We are interested in reliable, well-documented research across:

- Full-chain mobile (iOS & Android)
- Modern Windows (RCE / LPE)
- Browser full chains
- Enterprise, cloud, VPN and security products
- Weaponized research against recent and emerging vulnerabilities

Submissions are handled confidentially.  
Terms are agreed in writing before any technical material is shared.
